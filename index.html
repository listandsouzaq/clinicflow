The workflow

Simple for the clinic team. Clear for the patient.

01 · ENQUIRY

Patient contacts you

Website, Google, Instagram, WhatsApp or a missed call.

02 · RESPONSE

Patient gets a response

The enquiry is acknowledged and the right next step is presented.

03 · FOLLOW-UP

Lead stays organised

Team members can see status, notes, follow-ups and appointment progress.

04 · APPOINTMENT

Move toward booking

Qualified enquiries are guided toward the clinic's appointment process.
